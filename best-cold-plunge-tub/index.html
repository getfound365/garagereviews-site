Frequently asked questions

Do I need a chiller, or is ice enough?

Ice is enough to start: a tub-only pick like either NordPod plus cold tap water and bags of ice gets you plunging for the lowest cost. A chiller kit costs more and needs a suitable outlet and airflow, but it keeps the water cold day after day without ice runs.

What is the cheapest cold plunge tub here?

The Lifepro NordPod XL at $99.99 on Walmart (listing snippet, Nov 20, 2026). The Cold Pod 88-gallon is another budget ice-only option at about $130, per The Review Store's September 2026 guide.

How cold do these get?

The chiller kits state their floor: the JUGRFIT cools to 37°F and the 1stplunge to 42°F, per their makers. Ice-only tubs depend on how much ice you add and the room temperature — there is no set floor.

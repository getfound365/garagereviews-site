Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

The short version

The Sauna Finder doesn't collect, send, or store any personal information. This site doesn't ask you to create an account or give us personal details to browse it.

The Sauna Finder

Everything you enter in the Sauna Finder — location, heat source, space, and budget — stays in your browser. It is used only to build the shopping links shown on the page, and it is never sent to Garage Reviews. The finder has no photo feature: we don't receive or store photos from it. The handoff text the finder prepares is copied by you into ChatGPT yourself; we never receive it.

Ask Garage Reviews

If you submit a question through Ask Garage Reviews, your question is sent to our server and processed by an AI model to generate an answer from our published reviews. We don't store your questions.

Email

If you email us at garagereviews1@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most websites, standard server logs may record technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We don't use that information to identify individual visitors.

Links to retailers

Clicking a link to Amazon, eBay, Sweaty Yeti, or another retailer takes you to that retailer's site, and their privacy policy applies from there. Those retailers may set their own cookies. Garage Reviews doesn't run advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

Children

This site is for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions

Questions about this policy: garagereviews1@gmail.com.

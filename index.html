Build your outage plan

A generator is only one part of the plan. Know your loads, choose your fuel, and decide how power will reach your appliances before the weather changes.

  1. List what matters most

    Start with your refrigerator, sump pump, lights, and phone chargers. Check how much power each needs while running and when turning on — starting watts are higher than running watts.

  2. Plan for a longer outage

    Fuel, oil changes, cooling breaks and a safe outdoor location all belong in the plan. Run a generator at least 20 feet from the house, exhaust pointing away, never in a garage or under a porch roof — carbon monoxide is odorless and kills every storm season.

  3. Choose the right connection

    Use suitable outdoor cords for approved appliances, or have an electrician install a safe house connection. Never send generator power into a wall outlet.

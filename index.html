Site Overview

Garage Reviews is a hands-on video review site for garage, shop, and home gear. We cover generators, workbenches, tools, yard equipment, hunting gear, and home backup power — plus head-to-head comparisons — and every review starts the same way: we buy it, unbox it, fire it up, and run it hard on camera.

No spec-sheet summaries and no paid placements. You see the cold start, the real load, the noise, and the runtime for yourself, then get a straight verdict: what's good, what's not, and who should buy it. New tests post to the site and our YouTube channel every week.

Quick Answers

How does Garage Reviews test products?

Every product is unboxed, fired up, and run hard in a real garage on video — start to finish — before we say a word about it. If it can't handle real loads and real work, we say so.

What does Garage Reviews cover?

Generators, workbenches, tools, yard equipment, and hunting gear — plus head-to-head comparisons.

How does Garage Reviews make money?

Garage Reviews is reader-supported. When you buy through links on the site, through retailer affiliate programs, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Reviews reflect our own testing and opinions and are not paid placements.

What's the #1 generator safety rule?

Never run a generator indoors or in an enclosed space, and keep it at least 20 feet from your home. Always follow the manufacturer's instructions and safety warnings.

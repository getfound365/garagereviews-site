Frequently asked questions

What is a dual-fuel generator?

One that runs on gas or propane. Dual fuel means you are not stuck if one fuel runs out — and propane stores longer than gas, which matters in a long outage.

Why under $1,000?

That is the band where the roster's three dual-fuel models sit: $799, $949, and $999. Nothing over $1,000 is in the roster yet.

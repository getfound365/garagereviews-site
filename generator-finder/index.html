Frequently asked questions

What size generator do I need?

Add up the running watts of everything you will run at the same time, then add the highest starting watts of any one appliance — motors and compressors need extra watts to start. The finder does the math for you: answer what you need to power and it matches from 33 verified models, from 900 running watts (Pulsar G1200SG) to 10,500 running watts (DuroMax XP13000HX).

What size generator do I need to run my whole house?

Square footage alone does not size a generator — what you run does. A fridge, lights, furnace fan and phones need far less than central AC plus a well pump running at once. Answer the finder's three questions and it sizes by your actual load: for essentials through a transfer switch, the Westinghouse WGen9500DF (9,500 running watts) is the home-backup pick in our roster.

What size generator do I need for a 1,500-watt heater?

At least 1,500 running watts, plus headroom for anything else running at the same time — a heater is a continuous load, so size above it, not at it. In our roster, the Westinghouse iGen2500 (2,200 running watts) covers a 1,500-watt heater with room to spare.

Best cellular trail camera

The head-to-head buyers ask most is Moultrie Edge 3 vs Tactacam Reveal X 3.0, and the roster holds both. The Moultrie Edge 3 shoots 40MP photos with a 0.5-second trigger and a 100 ft detection range — $69.99 for a single camera, $149.00 for the 2-pack — on a $9.99-a-month plan per camera. The Tactacam Reveal X 3.0 shoots 4K photos with a 0.5-second trigger, low-glow IR to 96 ft, built-in GPS and 8GB of storage — $115.99, on a REVEAL plan from $5 a month. The roster also holds the previous-generation Tactacam Reveal X 2.0 at $79.00 and the Moultrie Edge 4 at $129.00.

Frequently asked questions

Is Moultrie or Tactacam better?

It splits on what you value. The Moultrie Edge 3 single is $69.99 against the Tactacam Reveal X 3.0 at $115.99 — $46 less per camera — with 40MP photos and a 100 ft detection range. The Reveal X 3.0 costs less to run, with REVEAL plans from $5 a month against Moultrie’s $9.99, and adds built-in GPS and 8GB of storage. Buy the Edge 3 for the cheaper camera; buy the X 3.0 for the cheaper plan and the GPS.

What is the best value cellular trail camera?

The Moultrie Edge 3 single at $69.99 — the cheapest camera in this head-to-head with a live review — but count the plan: $9.99 a month per camera, every month you run it. The Tactacam Reveal X 2.0 at $79.00 is the cheaper-to-run alternative, with REVEAL plans from $5 a month.

EcoFlow vs Jackery

At the 1,000 Wh tier the two brands meet head-on in the table below. The EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classical 1000 is $438.90 in stock at Walmart (1,024 Wh, 1,800 W, 4.5 stars from 2,229 ratings). The closest Jackery, the Explorer 1000 v2, is $529.99 on eBay and in stock there (1,070 Wh, 1,500 W, 4.9 stars from 299 ratings) — out of stock at Walmart. Jackery’s cheapest in-stock 1,000 Wh-class option is the Explorer 1000 Plus (Restored) at $449.00.

Below that tier, the EcoFlow RIVER 2 Pro is $349 back in stock, while the Jackery Explorer 300 LiFePO4 is $309 in stock. On count, Jackery has the deeper bench here — eight models in the table against EcoFlow’s three. Read the full reviews: EcoFlow DELTA 3 Classical 1000 and Jackery Explorer 1000 v2.

Best solar generator for home backup

The home-backup class in the table is the 2,000+ Wh tier. The biggest in-stock pick is the Jackery HomePower 3000 v2 — 3,072 Wh, 3,600 W output, $1,199 (was $1,699), in stock. The true solar-paired pick is the Jackery Explorer 2000 v2 + 2×100W Mini Solar Panels — 2,042 Wh, 2,200 W, $999, in stock, sold with two 100W mini solar panels. The budget 2,000 Wh pick is the OUKITEL P2001 Plus at $659.99 (2,048 Wh, 2,400 W, in stock). The standalone Explorer 2000 v2 at $1,349 is out of stock — the solar bundle is the buyable way to get that station. The 1,000 Wh tier — DELTA 3 at $438.90 and the 1000 Plus (Restored) at $449.00 — covers short outages and essentials, not multi-day home backup.

What is the best solar generator for home backup?

The Jackery HomePower 3000 v2 — 3,072 Wh, 3,600 W output, $1,199 in stock — the biggest station in the table. The solar-paired pick is the Jackery Explorer 2000 v2 + 2×100W Mini Solar Panels at $999, in stock. The budget 2,000 Wh pick is the OUKITEL P2001 Plus at $659.99.

Can a solar generator power a whole house?

No — not a whole house on battery alone. The biggest station in our table is the Jackery HomePower 3000 v2 at 3,072 Wh and 3,600 W output, in the same class as the generator finder’s 3,000 W essentials tier (fridge, lights, TV, phones). Whole-house backup with central air conditioning needs a fuel generator — the roster’s whole-house pick is the Westinghouse WGen9500DF at 9,500 running watts.

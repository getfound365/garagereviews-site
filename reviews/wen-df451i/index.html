Who it’s for

RVers and homeowners who put quiet first: 58 dBA at quarter load is about as loud as a window air conditioner, and the TT-30R outlet plugs straight into a 30-foot camper, which is exactly what Rose runs off it. The 2.2-gallon tank gives 8.5 hours at 50% load on gasoline or 9 hours on a 20-pound propane tank, and the auto fuel selection switches between the two without a manual changeover. It is not for anyone who needs it this week — it is out of stock — and it is not a whole-home unit: 3,500 running watts covers a fridge, lights, a furnace fan, and a camper, not central air.

Setup

Keyless electric start at the press of a button, with a recoil pull starter as backup, and a fuel shut-off that lets the carburetor run dry for storage — the feature that keeps a generator starting next season. The auto fuel selection switches between gasoline and propane on its own. The panel carries two 120V household receptacles, one RV-ready TT-30R, one 12V DC receptacle, and two 5V USB ports, plus a digital fuel and load display and an Eco-Mode switch. No CO sensor is listed in the published specifications, so the safety rules are on you: outdoors only, at least 20 feet from the house, exhaust away from windows and doors, never in a garage, and a battery CO alarm in the house whenever a generator is running.

Who it's for

Homeowners with a portable generator (7,500W or less, floating neutral, 120/240V L14-30 outlet) who want to power house circuits in an outage without extension cords — and who will pay an electrician for a code-correct install. Not for: whole-home backup (30-amp ceiling), bonded-neutral generators, or anyone looking for a DIY plug-and-play install.

Performance in real use

Buyers report the kit does what it says: clean switching between utility and generator, with the two analog wattmeters letting them balance the load across both legs. The 4.8-star average from 132 ratings is the highest in this batch, and the review pool is deep enough to trust the trend. The recurring buyer advice is to have a licensed electrician do the install and to size the generator to the circuits you actually switch — the 7,500W / 30-amp ceiling is a hard limit, not a suggestion.

Setup

Per Reliance: a licensed electrician wires the 310CRK into the home load center, mounts the included 30-amp power inlet box outside, and connects the 10-ft L14-30 cord from the generator to the inlet. In an outage, plug the generator in, start it, and flip the transfer switches from LINE to GEN for the circuits you need. The two analog wattmeters show the load on each leg so you can balance it. This is not a DIY install — the listing and Reliance both call for a licensed electrician.

How it compares

There is no comparable transfer switch in this batch — the other nine products are generators and power stations, and this is the kit that connects them to a house. The right comparison is to the generators it pairs with: a 7,500W-capable unit like the Pulsar G12KBN (9,500 running watts) exceeds the 310CRK’s 30-amp ceiling, so it would run through the switch at partial load; a mid-size unit like the Westinghouse WGen9500DF is the classic whole-home pairing. Frequently asked questions

What does the 310CRK do?

It lets you run up to 10 home circuits from a portable generator during an outage — flip the switches from utility to generator, no extension cords through windows.

Do I need an electrician to install it?

Yes. The 310CRK wires into your home’s load center. Reliance and the listing both call for a licensed electrician; this is not a DIY install.

Which generators work with it?

Floating-neutral portable generators up to 7,500 watts / 30 amps, with a 120/240V L14-30 outlet. Bonded-neutral generators need a different setup — check your generator’s manual.

What are the wattmeters for?

Two round analog wattmeters show the load on each leg, so you can balance the generator and avoid overloading one side.

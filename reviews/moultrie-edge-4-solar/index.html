Who it's for

Anyone who wants the Edge 4 camera on a stand they rarely visit — the solar panel and lithium pack mean no battery runs. Performance in real use

Everything in the Edge 4 applies here — same 40MP sensor, 1080p FHD video, 0.4s trigger, and 100 ft detection and flash range — with one addition: the integrated adjustable solar panel feeding the rechargeable lithium pack, so there are no AA batteries to buy, carry, or change. That is the whole $0.99 premium over the Edge 4, and it is worth it if the camera sits where the panel sees sun. Setup

Strap it to a tree at about knee-to-chest height, facing north if you can — sun glare is the enemy of every trail camera — and angle the integrated solar panel toward the sun. Aim it with Live Aim on your phone before you walk away, and check the pack level in the app after the first week. Activate the Moultrie plan (from $9.99 a month) before you leave it in the woods, and test it: walk past it once and check that the photo lands on your phone. And remember the running cost: the plan is per camera, so two cameras means two plans.

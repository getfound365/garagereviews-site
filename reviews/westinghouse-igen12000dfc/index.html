Performance in real use

The 457cc engine makes 12,000 peak / 9,000 running watts on gasoline and 11,000 / 8,100 on propane, with a 7.9-gallon tank and up to 19 hours at 25% load on gas — about 0.42 gallons per hour, one of the better fuel-efficiency figures in its class. At 64 dBA it is quieter than the open-frame generators in this batch, and under 3% THD means sensitive electronics are safe on it. The control panel carries 4 AC outlets including a 50-amp outlet, and it is transfer-switch ready. Remote start comes with a key fob. Buyers report it starting first try and running a house; one buyer at high altitude reports reduced output and is installing the high-altitude kit.

Setup

Recoil, electric, and remote start — the key fob is included. At 189 pounds it is light for its class, but still a two-person lift or a ramp job. Break it in before the outage: run it at varying loads for the first hours, change the oil on schedule, and test every outlet — the newest buyer did exactly that and reports all outlets working. If you live at altitude, order the high-altitude kit with it; the standard jetting loses output above roughly 7,000 feet.

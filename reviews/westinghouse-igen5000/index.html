The verdict

Buy it if you want the 5,000-watt Westinghouse inverter for $50 less than the CO-sensor version and you do not need the sensor. At $849 it is the cheapest 5,000-watt inverter in this batch, with the same 224cc engine, the same 3.4-gallon tank, and the same remote start as the iGen5000c. Buyers give the listing 4.7 stars from 16,250 ratings — a family aggregate, so read it as the iGen5000 line's record, not this exact unit's — and the words that keep showing up are “easy set up,” “very quiet,” and “remote start.” One buyer: “I just received my generator and it worked as advertised.”

The honest catches: there is no CO sensor on this model — if the sensor matters to you, the iGen5000c is $899 — and the review count is shared across the family, so the 16,250 figure overstates this exact listing's base. At 104.7 pounds it is not a one-person carry. If you want propane, the Champion 201319 at $999 is the move.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants the 5,000-watt Westinghouse inverter at the lowest price in the batch and does not need a CO sensor or propane. Skip it if the CO sensor matters to you: the iGen5000c is $50 more. Skip it if you need propane: this model is gas-only.

Performance in real use

The 224cc engine makes 5,000 peak / 3,900 running watts, with a 3.4-gallon tank and up to 18 hours at 25% load — about 0.19 gallons per hour. At 52 dBA it is quiet for its class, and under 3% THD means sensitive electronics are safe on it. The control panel carries 3 AC outlets including the TT-30R RV outlet, plus a digital display. Remote start comes with a key fob — buyers call it out by name: “key fob works great,” “very happy with generator.” One buyer notes the electric start did not work until the battery charged overnight — charge the battery before the first start.

Setup

Recoil, electric, and remote start — the key fob is included. Charge the battery overnight before the first electric start: one buyer reports it would not start electronically until the battery charged. At 104.7 pounds, plan a ramp or a second person to load it. Break it in before the outage and test the remote start from inside the RV — the fob is the feature buyers praise most.

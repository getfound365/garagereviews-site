The verdict

Buy it for the twin-pack price. Two Flex-M cameras for $79.99 is about $40 a camera, and a single Tactacam Reveal X 2.0 costs $79.00 on its own — so this pack covers two spots for the price of one rival camera. The hardware is solid for the money: 28MP photos, 720p video with sound, a 0.4-second trigger, 90 feet of detection and flash with low-glow LEDs, dual-SIM connectivity that picks the strongest of AT&T or Verizon, GPS, and Constant Capture, which keeps taking photos while the camera transmits. Every SPYPOINT cellular camera includes 100 free photos a month, and SPYPOINT's own plan page lists $5 a month for 250 photos, $10 for 1,000 HD photos, and $15 for unlimited, with annual billing cheaper.

The honest catch is the 3.9-star rating. Of the newest 25 reviews, 6 are 1-star, and the recurring complaint is not the picture quality — it is compatibility: the wrong SD card or battery brand and the camera will not work right, and one buyer reports the SIM cards missing from the box on delivery. Buy a SanDisk microSD card with it, use fresh name-brand batteries, and test both cameras at home before you hang them in the woods. If you want a single proven camera instead, the Flex-M Solar Bundle is the same camera with a solar panel for $99.99 and 4.4 stars.

Who it's for

Hunters and property owners who want two cellular cameras — two stands, two gates, two food plots — for the price of one rival camera, and who are comfortable with a free 100-photos-a-month plan per camera. Skip it if you need 1080p video: the Flex-M shoots 720p, and that is the weakest spec here. Skip it if you will not buy the right SD card and batteries: the 1-star reviews are mostly compatibility failures, not broken cameras. And skip it if you want one camera with solar included — the Flex-M Solar Bundle is $99.99 with 4.4 stars.

Performance in real use

On the spec sheet this is a capable budget cellular camera, twice over. The 28MP photos and 720p video with sound go straight to the SPYPOINT app over dual-SIM cellular — no WiFi needed — and the 0.4-second trigger with a 90-foot detection and flash range is fast enough for deer at a trail or a feeder. Constant Capture means the camera keeps shooting while it sends, so a burst of activity does not cost you the second animal. The low-glow LEDs light the night without the visible red glare of older cameras, and the GPS tagging plus BUCK TRACKER species filtering in the app are real scouting tools, not gimmicks. What the reviews add is the caveat: the newest 25 reviews split 8 five-star against 6 one-star, and the one-star side is dominated by setup and compatibility failures — wrong SD card, wrong batteries — rather than dead hardware. Get the card and batteries right and the camera does what the spec sheet says.

Setup

Activate each camera in the SPYPOINT app — the free plan gives 100 photos a month per camera with no contract. Insert a microSD card (SanDisk or SPYPOINT brand — buyers report other cards failing) and load fresh name-brand AA batteries or a LIT-22 rechargeable pack. Format the card and take a test photo from the on-camera buttons before you leave, and check the signal at the spot: dual SIM picks the strongest of AT&T or Verizon, but no signal means no photos on your phone until you pull the card. If you want more than 100 photos a month on a camera, add a paid plan for that camera — $5 a month for 250 photos, $10 for 1,000 HD photos, $15 for unlimited, or $4/$7/$10 a month on annual billing. One plan per camera; the free plan does not cover two cameras on one subscription.

Is there really a free plan?

Yes — every SPYPOINT cellular camera includes 100 free photos a month, with no contract and no activation fee. Paid plans are $5 a month for 250 photos, $10 for 1,000 HD photos, and $15 for unlimited; annual billing is $4, $7, and $10 a month. One plan per camera.

Does the Flex-M need WiFi?

No — it uses True Dual SIM cellular (AT&T and Verizon) to send photos straight to the SPYPOINT app, so it works where there is no WiFi. It still needs cellular coverage where you mount it.

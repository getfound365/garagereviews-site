The verdict

The iGen4500DF is our pick for outage essentials. 4,500 peak watts on gas covers a fridge, lights, furnace fan, and phones at once, and dual fuel means you are not stuck if one fuel runs out. Remote start means you can fire it up from inside instead of standing in the weather.

The video's one hard rule: run it at least 20 feet from the house, exhaust pointing away, never in a garage or under a porch roof. Carbon monoxide is odorless and kills every storm season. The video also says plainly when to skip this generator — watch the last two minutes for that part.

The verdict

The iGen4500DF is our pick for outage essentials. 4,500 peak watts on gas covers a fridge, lights, furnace fan, and phones at once, and dual fuel means you are not stuck if one fuel runs out. Remote start means you can fire it up from inside instead of standing in the weather.

The video's one hard rule: run it at least 20 feet from the house, exhaust pointing away, never in a garage or under a porch roof. Carbon monoxide is odorless and kills every storm season. The video also says plainly when to skip this generator — watch the last two minutes for that part.

Who it's for

Anyone who needs the outage essentials covered — fridge, lights, furnace fan, phones — without stepping up to a whole-home unit. Dual fuel suits anyone who doesn't want to depend on a single fuel, and the remote start suits anyone who would rather fire it up from inside than stand in the weather. At 98 lb it is a two-person lift or a ramp job, so plan the loading before the storm, not during it.

Performance in real use

4,500 peak / 3,700 running watts on gas, 4,050 peak / 3,330 running watts on propane — enough for the essentials at once. Up to 18 hours on a tank of gas at 25% load, 14 hours on propane. At 52 dBA it is quiet for a generator, and total harmonic distortion under 3%. Outlets are a TT-30R 30A RV outlet plus household duplex outlets, fed by a 224cc engine and a 3.4-gallon tank.

Setup

Fill the 3.4-gallon tank with gas, or hook up a propane tank. Start it with the push button or the included remote — recoil is the backup. Then the rule from the video: at least 20 feet from the house, exhaust pointing away, never in a garage or under a porch roof. Carbon monoxide is odorless, and the video is blunt about it for a reason.

How it compares

Against the WGen9500DF at $999, the iGen4500DF gives up whole-home power — 9,500 running watts and a transfer-switch outlet — but it is far more portable at 98 lb against 211 lb, and far quieter at 52 dBA against an open-frame design. Against the iGen4000DFc at $799, it costs $150 more and gives 500 more peak watts, and it is the one you can actually buy right now.

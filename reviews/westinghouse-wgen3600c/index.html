Who it's for

Small-RV owners who need a TT-30R outlet and about 3,600 running watts without paying inverter prices, and homeowners covering a fridge, freezer, lights, and chargers in an outage. Not for sensitive electronics as a primary load — total harmonic distortion is up to 23% — or anyone who needs quiet campground operation. This is an open-frame unit.

Performance in real use

Buyers in the newest 25 reviews consistently report first-pull starts and straightforward power delivery. “EASY setup,” wrote one buyer (Nov 14, 2026), after installing the wheels, adding oil and gas, and getting “120 volt output as expected” on the first pull. RV use is the dominant theme: “Perfect for small RV,” wrote Sarah (Nov 17, 2026), with the warning that it is a “heavy item so be warned that it will take some getting used to when moving it around.” Multiple buyers praise the included startup kit — one noted the box included “a .6 qt bottle of oil for the full first crankcase filling” plus a funnel (FG, Nov 15, 2026). The single 1-star review in the newest 25 is a shipping-damage complaint (unit dropped, bent frame), not a running failure. No buyer in the newest 25 reports a no-start or underpower problem.

Setup

Per the manual and buyers: attach the wheel kit, add the supplied 10W-30 oil (0.63 qt capacity), add unleaded gasoline (87–93 octane, up to 10% ethanol; no E15/E85), and pull-start. The manual specifies a break-in of no more than 50% of rated running watts for the first five hours, varying the load. Buyers repeatedly call the instructions good and the assembly kit complete.

Frequently asked questions

How many watts does the WGen3600c produce?

3,600 running watts and 4,650 peak watts, per the Westinghouse manual.

Does the WGen3600c have an RV outlet?

Yes — a 30-amp TT-30R RV receptacle, plus a 30-amp L5-30R twist-lock and duplex 20-amp household outlets.

Is the WGen3600c an inverter generator?

No. It is a conventional open-frame generator with an AVR and a brushed alternator; the manual lists total harmonic distortion of ≤ 23%.

Does it have a CO sensor?

Yes — the Walmart listing names it as a “Gas Powered Portable Generator with CO Sensor.”

What oil and fuel does it take?

SAE 10W-30 oil (0.63 qt capacity) and unleaded gasoline, 87–93 octane, with up to 10% ethanol. Do not use E15 or E85, per the manual.

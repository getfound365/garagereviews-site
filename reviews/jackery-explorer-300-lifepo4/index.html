The verdict

Buy it if you want the LiFePO4 Explorer 300 with the extension cord and you are comfortable with a thin review base. At $309 it is $20 more than the $289 standalone at Jackery direct — the 5m extension cord is the difference — and the battery is the point: LiFePO4 at 4,000+ cycles to 70%, against the discontinued NMC original's roughly 500 cycles. Buyers who have it call it “portable and powerful” and “works great,” and one runs a mobility scooter twice as far on it.

The honest catch is the review base: 15 Bazaarvoice reviews is not enough to settle anything, and two of them are 1-star delivery complaints — one buyer ordered expecting a solar panel and sent it back. Who it's for

Performance in real use

The 292Wh LiFePO4 battery (291.84Wh per Jackery) is rated for 4,000+ cycles to 70% capacity, with 300 watts of output, two AC outlets, 100W USB-C PD, two USB-A ports, and a 120W car port. At 7.5 pounds it is the lightest station in this batch. The 5m extension cord is the variant's difference: it puts the station where you need it at a campsite instead of at the outlet. Buyers use it camping, for an outdoor shower off-grid, and on a mobility scooter. The 5-year warranty is the longest in this batch.

Setup

Charge it to 100% before the first trip — from the wall, a car, or a solar panel (sold separately). The extension cord is in the box; the solar panel is not, whatever the listing photo suggests — read the “what's included” before you buy. Keep it between charges in storage and top it up every few months.

The verdict

Buy it for the live feed and the price. Two cameras with a 2.5K live video stream you can watch from your phone, 10x digital zoom, two-way audio, and a 4-camera view in the CloudEye 365 app — for $59.99, $30 a camera — is the cheapest live-view cellular setup in the roster. The hardware on paper is strong: 32MP photos, 940nm no-glow infrared, a 0.1-second trigger with human detection out to 65 feet, a 4W solar panel with a 7,800mAh battery rated to 365 days of continuous power, and IP66 waterproofing. The built-in SIM works on AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon with no WiFi needed, and without a plan the cameras still record to a microSD card.

The honest catch is the plan and the proof. The 7-day free trial is unlimited, but after that it is $19.99 a month per camera — $39.98 a month for the pair, $480 a year, which is eight times the price of the cameras. Longer plans cut the daily cost ($169 for 360 days per camera), but there is no free tier like SPYPOINT's 100 photos a month. And the owner evidence is almost nonexistent: 140 ratings on the Walmart page, but only 2 written reviews in Bazaarvoice, split 1-star and 5-star — the 1-star buyer calls the service unstable with poor video quality, the 5-star buyer says the live view works from the living room with no WiFi and no subscription. If you want owner proof first, the Campark TC32 is $34 for one camera with 75 ratings behind it.

Who it's for

Property, farm, and cabin owners who want a live camera feed on two spots for $59.99 and will pay for the data plan, and hunters who want to watch a feeder or a gate in real time instead of pulling SD cards. Skip it if you will not pay $19.99 a month per camera — without a plan, nothing reaches your phone. Skip it if you want owner proof before you pay: only 2 written reviews exist. And skip it if you need coverage outside the US — the built-in SIM works in the United States only.

Performance in real use

On paper this is the most capable cheap cellular camera in the roster. The 2.5K live feed runs over 4G with 10x digital zoom you control from the app, two-way audio so you can talk through the camera, and a four-camera view on one phone screen. The 0.1-second trigger with human detection out to 65 feet is the fastest trigger in this batch, and the 940nm no-glow infrared switches on automatically at night. The 4W solar panel and 7,800mAh battery are rated to 365 days of continuous power in sunlight, and the camera is IP66 waterproof. What is not verified is any of it in the field: with only 2 written reviews, the trigger, the night vision, and the app all rest on Campark's word and the Walmart listing text, not on buyer experience. One buyer does confirm the live view works with no WiFi coverage at all at the install site — the strongest single data point on this page.

Setup

Mount each camera with the included strap, angle the solar panel at the sun, and activate the built-in SIM in the CloudEye 365 app for 7 days of free unlimited data — no WiFi needed, but check that AT&T, T-Mobile, or Verizon has coverage at the spot first. Insert a microSD card up to 128GB for local storage (not included); without one, footage goes to the cloud only. Set your detection and privacy zones in the app, and pick a plan before the trial ends: 30 days is $19.99, 90 days $49.99, 180 days $99.90, 360 days $169.00, 720 days $259.00 — each camera needs its own plan, and you cannot share one plan between cameras. There are no long-term contracts and you can pause a plan; with a plan paused, the camera still records to the SD card, but nothing reaches your phone.

Who it's for

Anyone with a camera site a long walk from the truck who wants to stop changing batteries — and who accepts the subscription as part of the price. Skip it if the camera sits close to home: the Edge 2 Pro at $62 is sharper, faster, and $37.99 cheaper. Skip it if you need it this week: it arrives Dec 4–9.

Performance in real use

The 40MP sensor matches the Edge 2 Pro's resolution, and the 1080p video is a step down from the 2 Pro's 1440p but a step up from the original Edge's 720p. The 0.4-second trigger sits between the two (0.30s and 0.85s), and the 100 ft range matches the 2 Pro. The solar story is the point: a built-in adjustable panel feeds a 10.8V 2,600mAh rechargeable pack, and Field & Stream's reviewer found it held charge for weeks in partial shade — the panel works even when the sun is not direct. It is bulkier than the non-solar cameras, per Field & Stream, and it carries 8GB of built-in memory. It weighs 2 lb.

Setup

Strap it to a tree at about knee-to-chest height, and angle the built-in solar panel toward the sun — south-facing if you can, and clear of branches that shade it by midday. A panel in full shade is a decoration, not a charger. Activate the Moultrie Mobile plan before you leave it in the woods, and test it: walk past it once and check that the photo lands on your phone. It carries a 2-year warranty. And remember the running cost: the plan is per camera.

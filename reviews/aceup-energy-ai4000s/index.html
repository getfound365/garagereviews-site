Setup

Per Aceup: add SAE 10W-30 oil, add unleaded gasoline, and electric-start (or recoil-start as backup). The LED display shows runtime, fuel level, and output. The wheel kit and telescopic handle make it a roll-out unit at 50.7 lb. Break-in guidance mirrors the class: keep loads modest in the first hours.

Frequently asked questions

How many watts is the AI4000S?

4,000 peak watts and 3,200 running watts per the Walmart listing (4,000 peak). Aceup’s manual states 3,400 running watts — the listing figure is used on this page.

Does it have a CO sensor?

Yes — a CO sensor is listed among its safety features, with low-oil shutdown.

How long does it run?

Up to 8.5 hours at 25% load and 5.5 hours at 50% load, per Aceup’s materials, from a 1.6-gallon tank.

What is the warranty?

Disputed: the manual states a 1-year warranty, while Aceup’s site states 2 years. Confirm with Aceup before relying on either figure.

What can it run?

3,200 running watts covers a fridge, freezer, lights, chargers, and small power tools — one or two essentials at a time, not whole-home backup.

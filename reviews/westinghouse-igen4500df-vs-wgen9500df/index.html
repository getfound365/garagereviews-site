The verdict

Buy the iGen4500DF if you want the quietest generator that still runs the essentials — fridge, lights, furnace fan, phones — without an electrician and without waking the house. Who should buy which

The iGen4500DF is for the outage itself: one generator, no electrician, quiet enough to run near the house, and clean enough power (THD under 3%) for electronics. The WGen9500DF is for the house: a transfer switch, an electrician, and everything at once — well pump, AC, fridge, lights. It is 211 lb and open-frame loud; it is not a camping generator and it is not a bedroom-window generator.

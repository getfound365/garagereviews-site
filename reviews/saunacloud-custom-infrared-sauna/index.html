The verdict

This is the custom route, and the price is the proof of it: $11,000–$24,000 for a residential build, against $569.99 for a MUCHANT 1-person kit that arrives as a box and $29,700 for the Sweaty Yeti base model. What the money buys is fit — a cabin drawn for your exact room, with 3D renderings before you commit, instead of a standard box you squeeze into a corner. The 3,000+ installations since 2014 and the 7-year heater warranty are the track record behind the quote.

The honest catch is that you cannot comparison-shop this the way you shop a generator: there is no fixed price to check against another store, and the final number depends on your size, layout, and options. If you want a sauna this week for under a thousand dollars, buy the kit. If you are building or remodeling a dedicated sauna room and want the cabin to fit the space instead of the other way around, the quote is free — and that is the right way to find out what yours costs.

Who it’s for

Homeowners building or remodeling a dedicated sauna space who want exact dimensions, a glass front, and a heater system sized for the room — and who are comfortable with a quote process instead of a fixed price. The Atlas Outdoor is the one for an outdoor install: winter-rated to −20°F, while the indoor units are not outdoor-rated. It is not for anyone who wants a fixed price today, a box tomorrow, or a traditional rock-stove sauna — SaunaCloud builds infrared only, and everything is made to order.

The lineup

Five residential and commercial lines, all built to order. Every line is infrared only — there is no traditional sauna in the catalog.

Sierra — the value line

Compact single-bench indoor infrared for 1–2 people, 9–10.5kW on 240V/40A. Compact Sierras start around $8,000, the lowest entry point in the catalog. If your space is small and your budget is the constraint, this is the line the quote should start with.

Atlas — the main residential line

Premium 2–3 person custom cabin with a glass front, 15kW on 240V/60A, up to 8′×10′×8′. This is the cabin in the hero photo above — the one most home installs are built around.

Atlas One — the 1-person with red light

One person, 6kW on 240V/30A, 49.5″×52.25″×84″, with 300+ LEDs for near and far infrared. The smallest footprint and the smallest electrical ask in the range — still a dedicated 240V circuit.

Atlas Outdoor — the outdoor cabin

Outdoor infrared cabin for 2–3 people, 12kW on 240V/60A — the example build is 8′×6′×8′ at about 1,450 lbs, winter-rated to −20°F. The indoor units are not outdoor-rated, so the Outdoor is the only line for an outside install.

Commercial and VantaWave

Commercial rooms for hotels, spas, gyms, and clinics run $26,000–$72,000. The VantaWave is SaunaCloud’s own heater system (0.97 emissivity, up to 190°F, 7-year heater warranty) for new DIY or contractor-built rooms and retrofits — individual panels are not sold, and pricing is by quote for the room.

How the quote process works

There is no checkout. The process, as SaunaCloud describes it, runs like this:

  1. Free design. You describe the space — room size, indoor or outdoor, timeline — and SaunaCloud draws the sauna with 3D renderings for your exact dimensions.
  2. Itemized proposal in 24–48 hours. You get the build broken down line by line, so you can see what the money is for before you commit to anything.
  3. Build to order. The cabin is built for your space, not pulled from a warehouse shelf.
  4. Flat $999 shipping per order. One flat fee, whatever the build.

Two things to sort before you start: the electrical. Every line needs a dedicated 240V circuit (30A for Atlas One, 40A for Sierra, 60A for Atlas and Atlas Outdoor), so have an electrician confirm your panel first. And the space itself — measure twice, because a custom cabin is built for the numbers you give them.

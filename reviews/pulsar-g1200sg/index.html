2-stroke — read before buying. This generator has a 2-stroke engine and needs a 40:1 gas/oil mix (per the G1200SG manual). There is no separate oil fill — you mix 2-stroke oil into the gasoline. Running straight gas will damage it. One flag: some listings for the sibling PG1202SA say 30:1 — check the label on your unit.

The verdict

A $149 gamble, and the buyer record says so plainly. The newest 25 reviews are 11 five-star, 1 four-star, 5 three-star, 1 two-star, and 7 one-star — the widest split in the batch. The recurring complaint is that it doesn’t deliver its rated power: “Does not get even close to the 900 rated watts… Cut off constantly running half of that,” wrote Bob (Nov 17, 2026); “doesnt pull the power it should. we can barely run a box fan,” wrote Lindsey (May 29, 2026). The buyers who like it use it for very light loads and report it running fine. Best for: a buyer who understands 900 running watts is a phone-charger-and-lights machine, accepts the 2-stroke mix, and treats $149 as a disposable price — not for anyone counting on it for a fridge, a fan that matters, or a job that can’t fail.

Who it's for

Campers and tailgaters who need a few hundred watts for lights and charging, at the lowest possible price. Buyers who already own 2-stroke tools and are comfortable mixing fuel. Not for: refrigerators, freezers, power tools, medical devices, or anything where 900 watts is a floor rather than a ceiling. Not for buyers who don’t want to mix oil into their gas.

Performance in real use

The buyer record is genuinely split. Positive: “I have only ran it once and it runs good. It’s not loud like a diesel one I had,” wrote one buyer (Sep 26, 2026). Negative reports cluster on two themes — underpower and early failure. Bob (Nov 17, 2026, 1★): “Deserves a 0 not the obligatory 1… Does not get even close to the 900 rated watts… Cut off constantly running half of that.” Byron (Jul 31, 2026, 1★): “The generator does not work!” Ann (Dec 24, 2025, 1★): “I’ve had for 2mons and it broke down thank God for protection plans.” Beth (Apr 1, 2026, 1★): “HATE IT ALTOGETHER.” One 1-star review is a delivery complaint (wrong address, no refund yet — anonymous, Oct 20, 2026), not a product failure. The honest read: at this price, quality control and real output vary, and the 4.0-star average earns its place as the lowest in the batch.

Setup

Per the manual: mix unleaded gasoline with 2-stroke oil at 40:1, fill the 1-gallon tank, and recoil-start. There is no oil dipstick or separate oil fill — the oil goes in the gas. Use the correct ratio: too little oil starves the engine, too much fouls the plug and smokes. Keep a measuring bottle or pre-mixed fuel on hand; do not guess the ratio at a gas station.

Frequently asked questions

How many watts is the Pulsar G1200SG?

1,200 peak watts and 900 running watts, per the Pulsar manual. Note that buyers report it cutting out well below 900 watts in real use — size loads conservatively.

Does it need mixed gas?

Yes. It is a 2-stroke engine: mix 2-stroke oil into the gasoline at 40:1, per the G1200SG manual. There is no separate oil fill. Running straight gas will damage the engine.

What can it run?

Realistically, lights, phone chargers, and small electronics — a few hundred watts. Buyers report it struggling with even a box fan. Do not buy it for a fridge, freezer, or power tools.

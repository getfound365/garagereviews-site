Anyone who camps or runs an RV and wants dual-fuel flexibility with quiet inverter power — 62 dB is about normal speech — and who can wait for it to come back in stock. Skip it if you need a generator this week: it is out of stock. Skip it if you will be lifting it alone and the weight matters: the sources disagree between 78.5 and 91 lb.

Performance in real use

The 224cc WEN engine makes 4,800 surge / 4,000 running watts on gasoline and 4,320 / 3,600 on propane, with a 1.9-gallon tank and about 7 hours at 50% load on gas (12 hours on propane, per GearRanking). At 62 dB at quarter load it is quiet for its class. Consumer Reports lab-tested this model and called its power delivery “more than enough” with “especially clean power” — the kind of third-party check most generators in this batch do not have. Push-button start (keyless electric with a recoil backup) means no pull cord on a cold morning. It carries 6 outlets, per the Walmart listing.

Setup

Push-button start with a recoil backup — no pull cord unless the battery is flat. At 78.5 lb (or 91 lb, if Consumer Reports is right — see the note above), plan the lift: wheels and a handle help once it is on the ground, but do not plan to carry it alone up a truck ramp. Break it in on gasoline or propane before the outage — the first run on a new fuel is the wrong time to learn the fuel selector. A 3-year warranty covers it, per GearRanking.

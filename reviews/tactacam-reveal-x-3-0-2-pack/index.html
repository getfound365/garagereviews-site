Who it's for

Hunters and property owners who want two cellular cameras — two stands, two gates, two food plots — in a single order, and who are already in the REVEAL app. Skip it if price per camera matters: buy two singles at $84.99 each ($169.98) or Tactacam's own two-pack at $159.99 instead. Skip it if you will not pay for a data plan on each camera — without a plan, the cameras cannot send photos over cellular.

Performance in real use

On the spec sheet this is the outgoing generation done right: 16MP photos (4K per Walmart's listing; B&H lists an 8.3MP native sensor), 1080p FHD video in 10/15/30-second clips, a 0.5-second trigger (B&H lists 0.4s), low-glow IR flash to 96 ft at 850 nm, a 45-degree detection angle with a 60-degree field of view, and multi-carrier LTE with an integrated SIM that picks the strongest signal. The 8GB of built-in storage means no SD card is required to start, and the 2-inch color display plus the REVEAL app handle setup and aiming. What the reviews add is the reliability story: of the newest five reviews, four are 5-star, and the recurring theme is longevity — buyers who have burned through other brands keep coming back to Tactacam.

Setup

Activate each camera in the REVEAL app — the plan is per camera, not per pack: Starter $5 a month ($55 a year) for 250 photos, Intermediate $8 ($85) for 500, Pro $13 ($120) for unlimited. Load 12 AA batteries (not included) or a rechargeable lithium cartridge, and use the 2-inch display or the app to aim and take a test photo before you leave. The cameras need cellular coverage where you mount them — multi-carrier LTE picks the strongest of the major networks, but no signal means no photos on your phone until you pull the card. If you want HD downloads and on-demand photos, add Xtra: $4 a month for 1 camera, $7 for 2, $9 for 3 or more, one flat rate covering all cameras on the account. Live View is a separate $6 a month per camera — Tactacam's current product pages list it for the 4.0 models, so confirm in the REVEAL app whether the X 3.0 supports it.

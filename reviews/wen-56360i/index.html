Performance in real use

The 149cc WEN engine makes 3,600 surge / 2,900 running watts, with a 1.6-gallon tank and under 1.2% THD — clean enough for a laptop or a modern furnace board. It carries two 120V outlets, a TT-30R RV outlet, a 12V DC outlet, and two USB ports. At 65 dB at quarter load it is the loudest of the WEN inverters in this batch — quiet for a generator, but not as quiet as the 62 dB DF480iX or the 52 dBA Westinghouse inverters. Runtime is contested on the listing itself (see the note above): 3 hours in the spec table, “up to 5 hours at half load” in the description.

Setup

Pull-cord start only — no electric start, no remote. At 46 lb it is a genuine one-person carry to the campsite or the truck bed. The TT-30R outlet plugs straight into an RV shore-power cord. Break it in before the trip — the first pull on a cold morning is the wrong time to learn the choke. And check the oil before every run: a 149cc engine on a 1.6-gallon tank is a small machine doing real work.

Performance in real use

The 5-heater system — floor, full sidewalls, and calf heaters — brings a full-body sweat in 12–18 minutes, per the independent reviewer at HomeInDepth, who spent $1,999 of his own money and ran 8 sessions over 2 weeks. The temperature range is 68–140°F on a standard 110V outlet, so there is no electrician and no dedicated circuit to install. Setup

Assembly takes under 45 minutes, per the independent reviewer — two people makes it easier, but one can do it. It plugs into a standard 110V outlet: no electrician, no permit, no dedicated circuit. And measure twice — the listing's two dimension figures disagree (see the specs below), so leave clearance for the larger one.

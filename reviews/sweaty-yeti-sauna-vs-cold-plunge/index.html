The routine, step by step

From Sweaty Yeti's official contrast therapy post, verified Nov 20, 2026. The order is intentional — each step prepares your body for the next.

Step 1 — Sauna: 10 to 20 minutes at 160 to 195°F. Deep heat, circulation, heat-shock proteins. Sweaty Yeti notes its Sauna and Fit models heat quickly and hold temperature. Settle in, breathe through your nose, stretch lightly.

Step 2 — Cold shower: 30 to 60 seconds at 50 to 60°F. The first temperature drop — a quick reset without overwhelming your system. Slow, controlled breathing. The Sauna Plunge offers an outdoor shower as an upgrade for exactly this step.

Step 3 — Cold plunge: 1 to 3 minutes at 39 to 55°F. Full immersion, the biggest shift in the routine. Beginners start at 55°F for one minute, then lower the temperature and extend the time gradually. Sweaty Yeti's Plunge and Plus units hold a precise temperature.

Rounds: most people start with one full round and build to two or three as they adapt, three to five times a week. Morning sessions for energy, evening sessions to release tension.

Who it's for

People who train hard and want a repeatable recovery ritual at home — and who will use it three to five times a week. The combo makes sense for athletes, for households where two people run the routine together (the sauna seats 4–6), and for short-term rental owners who want a wellness feature guests actually use. It does not make sense as a curiosity: a $51,700 building you plunge in twice is the most expensive cold tub you will ever own.

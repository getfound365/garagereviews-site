Who it's for

Anyone starting out with cellular trail cameras who wants photos on their phone without pulling SD cards — and who accepts the $9.99-a-month plan as part of the price. Skip it if you will not pay a subscription: without a Moultrie Mobile plan, a cellular camera does nothing a $30 non-cellular camera cannot do. Skip it if you need solar power: the EDGE Solar at $99.99 below charges itself.

Performance in real use

The 40MP sensor takes sharper photos than the original Edge's 33MP, and the 1440p video with sound is a real step up from the original's 720p. The 0.30-second trigger is fast — a deer walking a trail gets caught, not missed — and the 100 ft detection and flash range covers a wide lane. Auto Connect LTE picks the strongest carrier signal, so it works wherever any of the major networks reach, and Smart Capture AI and Live Aim (per Field & Stream) help you aim it and sort what it sees. The 8GB of built-in memory (per Field & Stream) means no SD card to buy, lose, or pull in the rain.

Setup

Strap it to a tree at about knee-to-chest height, facing north if you can — sun glare is the enemy of every trail camera. Aim it with Live Aim on your phone before you walk away. Activate the Moultrie Mobile plan (from $9.99 a month) before you leave it in the woods, and test it: walk past it once and check that the photo lands on your phone. It carries a 2-year warranty. And remember the running cost: the plan is per camera, so two cameras means two plans.

The verdict

Buy it if you want the cheapest way into a pan-and-tilt cellular camera: $34.00 for a single TC32-4G Solar with a 2.5K live feed, solar charging, a 32GB microSD card in the box, and two-way audio. Buyers give it 4.2 stars from 75 ratings — a modest base, but a real one.

The honest catch is the review split: Bazaarvoice shows 3.69 stars from 13 reviews for this exact item, well below the 4.2-star Walmart figure, and the photo resolution is unsettled on the listing itself (46MP in one place, 2MP in another). Add the running cost — about $19.99 per 30 days for a 4G plan per camera after the 300MB trial — and the $49.99 camera costs about $290 in its first year with a plan. Buy it as a cheap live-feed experiment, not as a proven camera.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants to watch a gate, a driveway, or a deer trail live on their phone for the least money — and who accepts the $19.99-a-month plan as part of the price. Skip it if you will not pay a data plan: without one, the live feed stops after the 300MB trial. Skip it if you want a proven camera with hundreds of reviews: the Moultrie Edge 2 Pro at $62 below has 707 ratings, though it has no live feed and no pan and tilt.

Performance in real use

The 2.5K live feed is the headline: you can watch the camera's view live on your phone over 4G LTE and pan about 360 degrees and tilt 90 degrees from the Yukon app, instead of waiting for photos after the fact. Two-way audio lets you speak through the camera, and the six-light setup covers daytime color, nighttime infrared, and full-color night. The solar panel plus USB-C charging means no battery swaps, and the pre-installed EIOTCLUB SIM works on AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon. The honest gaps: the photo resolution is unsettled on the listing (see the note above), and the battery capacity and weather rating are not verified on the Campark pages read — so do not buy it for a megapixel number or an IP rating the maker never states.

Setup

Mount it where the solar panel sees sun — the Campark review notes it can be mounted upside down so water drains off — and angle it at the trail, gate, or driveway you want to watch. Activate the pre-installed EIOTCLUB SIM and use the 300MB of free data to test the live feed before you commit to a plan. The 32GB microSD card is already in the box for local storage. Test it before you walk away: open the live feed once and pan the camera from the Yukon app to confirm the connection holds.

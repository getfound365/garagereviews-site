Who it's for

Hunters who want the cheapest way into Tactacam cellular, and property owners who want a proven camera for remote monitoring. Skip it if you want the newest model — the X 3.0 is the current camera at $116.98. Skip it if you hate buying batteries: 12 AAs per camera adds up, and a solar panel or lithium pack is the long-term answer. And skip it if you will not pay a monthly plan — without one, it is not a cellular camera at all.

Performance in real use

The specs hold up against the current model: 16MP photos, a sub-half-second trigger, 96-plus feet of detection, and adjustable low-glow IR out to 96 feet. Hybrid Mode speeds up photo delivery, and the REVEAL app handles settings, mapping, and on-demand photo and video requests. It is rated IP66 weather-resistant, and buyers run it outdoors in all weather. The weak point in the reviews is not the pictures — it is the setup: a few buyers report a camera that did not work on arrival, a SIM card sent instead of an SD card, and customer service that did not answer the phone. Most buyers have no trouble, but test it at home before you hang it a mile from the truck.

Setup

Insert 12 AA batteries (lithium recommended in cold weather) and a full-size Class 10 U3 SD card, 16 to 32GB — neither is included. The camera has its own cellular SIM for sending photos; the SD card is separate, so do not confuse the two if the box contains a SIM. Activate the camera in the REVEAL app, pick a plan, and set the trigger, sensitivity, and photo resolution (up to 16MP) before you mount it. Mount it with the included strap, check the signal in the app, and take a test photo to confirm delivery before you leave it in the woods.

The verdict

It is back in stock at $1,349 — only 4 left — so buy the bundle if you want it, and don’t pay a markup for it elsewhere. On paper it is the right 2kWh home-backup station: 2,042Wh, 2,200 watts continuous, a sub-20ms UPS that keeps a router and computer online through a blackout, and a 0–100% AC recharge in about 1.7 hours. But the Walmart bundle is gone, Jackery’s own unit-only price is $999 on pre-order shipping by Dec 14, and the only buyer signal we have is a thin, sub-4-star Bazaarvoice base (3.4 stars from 43 ratings).

If you want the 2000 v2, buy the Walmart bundle at $1,349 — only 4 left. The Pecron F1000LFP at $369 is the smaller in-stock alternative; the Bluetti AC70 is out of stock at Walmart as of Nov 25, 2026. Jackery’s $999 unit-only pre-order is still the cheaper route if you already own a panel.

Who it’s for

Homeowners who want a full-day outage backup for the essentials — fridge, Wi-Fi, lights — in a unit one person can carry at 39.5 lb. At 2,200 watts continuous (4,400 surge) it handles a refrigerator, microwave, and coffee maker without tripping, per Jackery. It is not expandable, so if 2,042Wh is not enough, this is the wrong station — Jackery points those buyers at the expandable Solar Generator 2000 Plus instead. And Jackery is explicit that it is not for medical devices, life-support equipment, or data servers.

Performance in real use

The Walmart listing now shows 4.5 stars from 300 ratings (Nov 25, 2026); the Bazaarvoice pull returned no review text, only the 3.4-star average from 43 ratings. What we can say comes from Jackery’s own page: the sub-20ms switchover keeps a router, computer, and NAS online during a blackout; the 1.7-hour emergency AC recharge gets it from 0 to 100% fast enough to be ready for the next outage; and at under 30 dB in quiet mode (45 dB at full load) it is silent enough for a bedroom at night. The cells are automotive-grade LFP rated for 4,000 cycles — an 11-year theoretical service life — and Jackery claims about 95% charge retention after 12 months in storage. The bundle’s one 200W SolarSaga panel recharges it in daylight; Jackery’s own two-panel bundle is $1,649, which is the price anchor that makes the Walmart $1,349 bundle look fair when it is in stock.

Setup

Plug and play, per Jackery: no installation, the AC charging cable is in the box, and pass-through charging means you can run loads while it recharges. The app controls emergency charging mode and energy-saving mode (long-press the AC and main power buttons). It charges from a wall outlet, a 12V car cable, or solar; the bundle’s 200W panel plugs in with the included solar charging cable. Warranty is 3 years standard plus 2 years extended, through Jackery’s official site, branded third-party platforms, or authorized dealers.

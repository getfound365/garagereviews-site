The verdict

The SOL-PAK is the cheap fix for the most annoying part of running a cellular trail camera: the battery bill. For $54 you get a panel that trickle-charges a 3,000mAh battery all season, so the camera stops eating lithium AAs every few weeks. One buyer, OneBusyMomandDad, wrote in November 2026 that it is “way better than spending $$$ on lithium batteries only to have to replace them.”

The honest catches are the thin evidence and the size of the buffer. Sixteen ratings is barely a review base, so the 4.8-star figure is a weak signal, and 3,000mAh is a buffer that extends field life — it does not replace the camera’s own batteries outright. It also needs sun on the panel and a camera with a 12V DC jack. If your camera has no 12V jack, this does not fit.

Who it’s for

Anyone running a cellular or wireless trail camera through deer season who is tired of battery runs — especially Stealth Cam owners, since it is built for their 12V jack. It is not for cameras without an external 12V DC jack, or for a fully shaded setup where the panel never sees sun.

Performance in real use

The 12V amorphous solar panel continuously charges the internal 3,000mAh lithium-ion battery whenever it is exposed to sunlight, including low-light conditions such as cloudy weather and shade, per Stealth Cam. The 10-foot insulated metal cable reaches from the panel to the camera, and the secure-lock DC plug keeps the connection tight. The adjustable T-swivel brackets let you angle the panel for sun through the season, and the LED indicator shows the battery level at a glance. Buyers report it working beyond Stealth Cam too: Larry wrote in November 2026, “Love this solar/battery pack for my MUDDY camera.” Jennifer wrote in October 2026 that on her Stealth Cam 360, “power lasted 1 week while batteries were dead.”

Setup

Give the pack a base charge first through the micro USB port (cable not included), then mount it to the tree or post with the included strap, angle the panel with the T-swivel brackets for the best sun, and plug the secure-lock DC plug into the camera’s 12V jack. The Python lock pass-through takes a cable lock for security. You can link multiple panels through the 12V DC input if one is not enough.

How it compares

Against the Stealth Cam Deceptor Max at $108.12, the SOL-PAK is the accessory that keeps that camera — or any Stealth Cam cellular camera — running: buy the camera first, the SOL-PAK second. Against the Moultrie Edge 2 Pro at $62, the SOL-PAK costs about the same as a whole camera, which is the right way to judge it: it only pays if it saves you more than $54 in batteries over the season.

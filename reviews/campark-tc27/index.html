The verdict

Buy it if you want the cheapest solar cellular camera with a live pan-and-tilt feed and you are comfortable being the first owner to test it. At $49.99 it undercuts every other cellular camera in the roster, and the hardware on paper is strong: a 2.5K live stream you can pan 355 degrees and tilt 90 degrees from your phone, a 10,400mAh battery with a 6W solar panel rated to 10 months of standby, two-way audio, and AI animal detection free for the first year. Performance in real use

On paper this is the most capable $49.99 cellular camera in the roster. The 2.5K live stream runs over 4G with 355 degrees of pan and 90 degrees of tilt controlled from the UCon app, and it switches between three vision modes on its own: full-colour day, colour night with a white light, and invisible infrared black-and-white. The 0.5-second trigger is fast for the price, and the AI animal detection — free for the first year — sends alerts when wildlife is detected rather than every moving branch. The 10,400mAh battery plus the 6W solar panel is rated to 10 months of standby, and the camera is IP66 weatherproof. What is not verified is any of it in the field: no buyer has written in yet, so treat the spec sheet as a claim, not a result.

Setup

Charge the camera for about 10 hours before first use — Campark's own instruction. The SIM is built in and non-removable: activate it in the UCon app for 7 days of free data, then pick a plan. Insert a microSD card up to 128GB for local storage (not included); without one, footage goes to cloud storage only. Mount it with the included belt or the threaded tripod accessory, reset the camera and format the SD card through the app, and check the signal strength of AT&T, Verizon, or T-Mobile at the spot before you walk away — without coverage it still records to the card, but nothing reaches your phone.

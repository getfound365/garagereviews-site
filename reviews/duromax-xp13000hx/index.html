Performance in real use

The 500cc engine makes 13,000 peak / 10,500 running watts on gasoline and 12,500 / 10,000 on propane, with an 8.3-gallon tank and 17 hours at 25% load on gas, 13 hours on propane. The CO Alert system shuts the engine down on carbon monoxide buildup, and the 100% copper windings and sub-5% THD are the build-quality notes DuroMax leads with. Buyers report it running a whole house through outages — one for two years, “never failed to start/run for long durations.” The other side: 74 dB is loud, 240 pounds is a two-person-plus-a-ramp job, and the shipping warning is specific — “if your residence can't be reached by a TRAILER TRUCK be prepared to” arrange something, per one buyer.

Setup

Electric start — the 500cc engine turns over on a key, not a pull cord. At 240 pounds, plan a ramp and a second person; the wheels help once it is on the ground. Break it in before the outage and test the transfer-switch hookup with an electrician — this is a whole-house generator, and the hookup is the job. If you pre-order from DuroMax, confirm the delivery: buyers report trailer-truck freight, and a house a trailer cannot reach needs a plan before the truck leaves.

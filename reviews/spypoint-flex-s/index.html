Setup

Mount it where the solar panel sees sun — the integrated panel is the whole point, so a shaded trunk defeats it. The SIM is dual-SIM and preloaded: activate the camera in the SPYPOINT app, where the free plan gives 100 photos a month per camera with no contract. Use the on-camera buttons to format the microSD card and take a test photo before you leave, and check the signal at the spot — dual SIM picks the strongest major network, but no signal means no photos on your phone until you pull the card. Manage settings and firmware updates from home through the app. If you want more than 100 photos a month on a camera, add a paid plan for that camera — $5 a month for 250 photos, $10 for 1,000 HD photos, $15 for unlimited, or $4/$7/$10 a month on annual billing. One plan per camera.

Apartment dwellers and homeowners who need a few hours of fridge, lights, phone, and laptop power with no fumes or noise, and campers and tailgaters who want 1,800W of AC power without a gas generator. Setup

Per EcoFlow: charge from a wall outlet (0–80% in 45 minutes), solar (up to 500W), a car, or an EcoFlow Smart Generator. Pair with the EcoFlow app (Wi-Fi/Bluetooth) for Storm Guard mode, which auto-starts charging on extreme-weather alerts. No fuel, no oil, no pull cord — the setup is plug in and charge. Note the 10ms UPS switchover: plugged-in devices keep running through a power cut with no interruption EcoFlow rates fast enough for NAS systems and servers.

Frequently asked questions

Is the DELTA 3 Classic the same as the DELTA 2?

No. The DELTA 3 Classic replaced the DELTA 2 as EcoFlow’s current 1kWh station. It has 1,024Wh, 1,800W output, and 45-minute fast charging; the DELTA 2 is a different, older model.

How long does it take to charge?

0–80% in 45 minutes on AC, full in about 60 minutes, per EcoFlow. Solar (up to 500W) fills it in about 2.5 hours.

Can I add an extra battery to the DELTA 3 Classic?

EcoFlow’s spec table lists no extra-battery support for the Classic, unlike the standard DELTA 3 (expandable to 5kWh). Some retailer listings claim otherwise — the conflict is flagged above. Treat 1,024Wh as the ceiling unless EcoFlow confirms expansion for this exact model.

What can it run?

Any single 120V load up to 1,800W (3,600W surge; 2,600W with X-Boost) — microwaves, fridges, coffee makers, laptops, power tools. It cannot run 240V loads, central air, or a well pump.

Is it quiet?

EcoFlow rates it at ≤ 30 dB. Buyers describe it as quiet. We have not measured it ourselves.

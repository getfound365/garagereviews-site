Setup

Ready for use once placed on a bench or rolling base. The recessed handles on the ends let two people lift it onto a bench, and the key lock above the top drawer secures the drawers — though the listing does not say whether one key locks all six. Open the lid and there is a deep top compartment for bulky gear, held open by gas struts you can see in the photo, with a HART badge inside the lid. What the listing does not publish is a drawer-by-drawer size chart: no depth, no height per drawer, and it does not say whether the slides are ball-bearing or full extension. Measure your bench before you buy — the listing publishes no overall depth or height, only the 52-inch width in the name.

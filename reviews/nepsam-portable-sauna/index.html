Performance in real use

The 1,200W steamer with a 3L tank heats the tent to 107–140°F, with 9 power levels per the retailer listing — though the Bazaarvoice product name says 15, so that number is unsettled (see the note above). The 99-minute timer and remote let you run a session from the chair without getting up, and the folding chair is included, not an extra. The 2-person variant doubles the setup — dual steamers, two chairs, 48.5 by 37 by 71 inches — for $113.80 in Classic. The honest limit is the format: a fabric tent holds steam, not heat the way a wood cabin does, so sessions are steam sessions, not dry-heat sauna sessions.

Setup

Unfold the tent, set the steamer inside, fill the 3L tank, and plug it in — no electrician, no install, no dedicated circuit. Put the chair inside, keep the remote within reach, and set the timer before you sit down. Fold it away when the session is done: the box packs down rather than owning a corner of the room. And measure first if you are buying the 2-person variant — at 48.5 by 37 by 71 inches it needs a real patch of floor, not a closet.

The verdict

Buy it at $129.97 if you want the current REVEAL generation: 4K photos, 1080p video, simultaneous photo and video on the same trigger, Live View, and built-in GPS, for $12.99 more than the X 3.0. Buyers give it 4.7 stars from 28 ratings — the strongest-rated camera in the day’s set — and the Bazaarvoice cross-check is 4.77 from 13 reviews, close enough that the live stats call stays on. Battery life is the standout theme in the reviews: “batteries last lot longer than my other cameras,” wrote Byram (5 stars).

The honest catch is the subscription stack. The camera is $129.97, but the photos on your phone cost $5–$13 a month, Live View is another $6 a month per camera, and the Xtra add-on is $4–$9 a month if you want unlimited HD requests. Budget the plan before you budget the camera — and know the review base is still small at 28 ratings, with one 2-star report of a defective unit that needed a replacement.

Who it’s for

Hunters who want the newest REVEAL hardware and are already in — or ready to join — the REVEAL app ecosystem: 4K photos, 1080p video, and Live View for checking a stand in real time. It is the budget-conscious pick in Tactacam’s own 4.0 line: the Pro 4.0 adds dual image sensors, a side LCD, and selectable no-glow flash for more money, and the Pro 4.0 Solar adds the panel and FlexPack battery. It is not for anyone who wants photos without a monthly plan — without a REVEAL plan, the camera cannot send images over cellular.

Performance in real use

The nine text reviews are mostly enthusiastic. Byram (5 stars) calls it “top notch” and says the batteries last a lot longer than his other cameras. Serece (5 stars) owns three and “really like[s] them.” Faith (5 stars) reports “great images” and would recommend it; Wilton (5 stars) calls setup easy and pictures “very clear”; Amy (5 stars) reports it is “connecting great.” The two cautionary notes: Stephany (2 stars) had to call the company for a replacement after the original unit had defects and was still waiting for it, and Britt (5 stars) notes the camera “does great unless you live where cell phone coverage is not good then it seems to drain the batteries fast.” That last point matters — in poor coverage the radio works harder, so a FlexPack battery or solar panel is worth budgeting for a remote set.

Setup

Mount it, power it, activate it in the REVEAL app by scanning the camera’s QR code. Power is 12 AA batteries or the rechargeable FlexPack R5 (5,000 mAh) or R10 (10,000 mAh) packs, with solar panel compatibility. There is no built-in LCD screen — settings are adjusted in the app. Photos store to 8GB of internal memory with an optional SD card slot. Pick a plan before you deploy: Starter $5 a month (250 photos), Intermediate $8 a month (500), Pro $13 a month (unlimited) — and pause the plan in the off-season from the app or account portal if you only run cameras part of the year. Live View needs its own $6-a-month subscription per camera, and the Xtra add-on ($4 for one camera, $7 for two, $9 for three or more) covers unlimited HD photo and video requests.

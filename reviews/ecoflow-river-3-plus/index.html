Who it's for

Anyone who wants a light, quiet station for a CPAP, a router, or a laptop through a short outage — 10.4 lb, under 30 dB, and a sub-10ms UPS — and who can wait for it to come back in stock. Skip it if you need a station this week: it is out of stock with no Add to cart. Skip it if you need to run a space heater or a well pump: 600W continuous will not carry them.

Performance in real use

The LiFePO4 battery is rated for a 10-year lifespan to 80% capacity, with a 5-year warranty behind it. AC output is 600 watts continuous, and it recharges 0–100% in 1 hour from the wall (as fast as 1.5 hours on solar, per EcoFlow). The sub-10ms UPS switchover means a plugged-in router or CPAP keeps running through a blip, and at under 30 dB it is quieter than a conversation. At 10.4 pounds it is a one-hand carry to the campsite or the bedroom. The review base is thin — 58 ratings, and the Bazaarvoice cross-check is a single 5-star review — so treat the 4.6-star figure as early evidence, not a verdict from thousands of owners.

Setup

Charge it to 100% before the first trip — 1 hour from the wall — and leave it between 20% and 80% in storage if you will not use it for months. The app shows the remaining runtime; the color LCD shows the same. Check which variant you are buying before you pay: the listing names an EB600 bundle in its title, but only the 270 and the 270 + EB300 carry prices. Keep it dry and indoors: this is a battery, not a generator, and it belongs inside the house during an outage, not in the rain.

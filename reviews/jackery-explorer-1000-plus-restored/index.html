Performance in real use

The 1,264Wh LiFePO4 station makes 2,000 watts continuous with a 4,000-watt peak, takes up to 800W of solar input, and recharges from the wall in 1.7 hours. It expands to about 5kWh with up to 3 Battery Pack 1000 Plus units — but those expansion batteries are discontinued, so treat the expansion as a paper feature, not a plan. At 40 lb for the bundle it is a two-hand carry. The included 100W Mini solar panel is the reason this bundle exists: it charges the station off-grid, slowly, on a sunny day. There is no owner evidence to lean on — 5 ratings, no written reviews, no Bazaarvoice reviews — so the performance case rests on the spec sheet and on the 1000 Plus's record as a current model until September 2026.

Setup

Charge it to 100% before the first trip — 1.7 hours from the wall — and test it the day it arrives: a restored unit deserves a full charge-discharge check inside the return window. Set the 100W Mini panel in full sun, angled toward the midday sun, and expect a slow charge — 100W is a trickle against 1,264Wh. Keep it dry and indoors during an outage. And check the box on arrival: confirm it is the E1000P+100M bundle (station plus 100W Mini panel) and that the station is the refurbished unit the listing describes.

On its own merits, the original Edge is a solid first cellular camera: 33MP photos, 720p video with sound, an 0.85-second trigger, 80 ft of detection and flash, and 16GB of built-in memory with no SD card to lose. Who it's for

Anyone who wants the most-proven Moultrie cellular camera — 3,145 owners behind it — and who accepts the $9.99-a-month plan as part of the price. Skip it if you want the best camera for the money: the Edge 2 Pro at $62 beats it on resolution, video, and trigger speed for $5 less. Skip it if you will not pay a subscription: without a Moultrie Mobile plan, a cellular camera does nothing a $30 non-cellular camera cannot do.

Performance in real use

The 33MP sensor and 720p video with sound are a generation behind the 2 Pro's 40MP and 1440p, and the 0.85-second trigger is nearly three times slower — a walking deer can cross the frame before the shutter fires. The 80 ft detection and flash range is shorter than the 2 Pro's 100 ft. What the original has, and the 2 Pro does not yet, is depth of evidence: 3,145 ratings and 2,240 written reviews, with AI species detection and Auto Connect across all carriers (per GAT Daily). It works, it keeps working, and thousands of owners say so.

Setup

Strap it to a tree at about knee-to-chest height, facing north if you can — sun glare is the enemy of every trail camera. Activate the Moultrie Mobile plan before you leave it in the woods: Standard $9.99 a month or Unlimited $16.99 a month, per camera. Test it before you walk away: walk past it once and check that the photo lands on your phone. No SD card is needed — the 16GB of built-in memory holds the photos.

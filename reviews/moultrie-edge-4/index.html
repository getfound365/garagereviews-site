Who it's for

Anyone starting out with cellular trail cameras who wants the newest Moultrie — 40MP, AI buck detection, onX Hunt on the map — and who accepts the $9.99-a-month plan as part of the price. Skip it if you will not pay a subscription: without a Moultrie plan, a cellular camera does nothing a $30 non-cellular camera cannot do. Skip it if you want the sharpest video in the line: the Edge 2 Pro at $62 below shoots 1440p for $67 less.

Performance in real use

The 40MP sensor takes the same stills as the Edge 2 Pro, with 1080p FHD video — a step down from the 2 Pro’s 1440p, and the honest trade for the newest generation’s AI and connectivity. The 0.4-second trigger is fast — a deer walking a trail gets caught, not missed — and the 100 ft detection and flash range covers a wide lane. Auto-connect picks the strongest of the major US networks, and Moultrie AI with Buck Shot zooms to max resolution on bucks, while onX Hunt integration puts the camera on your hunt map. GPS and Live Aim help you place it and aim it from your phone, and the built-in memory means no SD card to pull in the rain. IP67 waterproofing means a downpour is not a problem.

Setup

Strap it to a tree at about knee-to-chest height, facing north if you can — sun glare is the enemy of every trail camera. Aim it with Live Aim on your phone before you walk away. Activate the Moultrie plan (from $9.99 a month) before you leave it in the woods, and test it: walk past it once and check that the photo lands on your phone. And remember the running cost: the plan is per camera, so two cameras means two plans.

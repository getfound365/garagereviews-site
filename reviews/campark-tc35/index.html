The verdict

Buy it as a $48.99 experiment — not as a proven camera. The TC35 is the cheapest cellular trail camera in this batch by $13, and it is the only one with a live 1080p feed, two-way audio, and a 0.1-second trigger at this price. Who it's for

Anyone who wants to try a cellular camera for the least money possible — $48.99 — and who accepts that the review base is 25 ratings, not 700. Skip it if you want a proven camera: the Moultrie Edge 2 Pro at $62 has 707 ratings and a real review record. Skip it if you will not pay a data subscription: after 7 days and 100MB free, the live feed stops without a plan.

Performance in real use

The 1080p live feed is the headline: you can watch the camera's view live on your phone over 4G LTE, not just receive photos after the fact. Starlight night vision with the F1.6 large-aperture lens is built for low light, and the 0.1-second trigger is the fastest in this batch — faster than the Moultrie 2 Pro's 0.30s. Two-way audio lets you speak through the camera, which no Moultrie in this batch offers. The solar panel and 7,800mAh battery keep it running without battery swaps. The photo resolution is contested on the listing itself — 14MP in the spec table, 15MP in the features list — so do not buy it for the megapixel number.

Setup

Strap it to a tree at about knee-to-chest height, facing north if you can, and angle the solar panel toward the sun. Activate the free 7 days of 100MB data first, then choose a plan before the free data runs out — the live feed stops without one. Bring your own microSD card (max 128GB, not included) if you want local storage. Test it before you walk away: trigger it once and check that the live feed opens on your phone.

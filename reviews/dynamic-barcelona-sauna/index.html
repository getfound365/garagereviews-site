Performance in real use

The 6 low-EMF carbon FIR panels heat the 39 × 36 × 73 in cabin to about 140°F maximum, per the retailer/manual specs — the figure is approximate, not a lab-measured number. Chromotherapy lighting and red light are built in, and Bluetooth covers audio. At 120V/15A it runs on an ordinary household circuit, which is the practical win over bigger cabins that need dedicated wiring. What we cannot tell you from the Walmart evidence is how owners rate the heat-up time or the panel layout — 10 ratings is not enough to separate a pattern from noise, and the dedicated reviews that exist (anysauna, mindbodydad, heatcoldlab, inhousewellness) are the better read before you buy.

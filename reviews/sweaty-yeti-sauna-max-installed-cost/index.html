Why the Max costs what it costs

The whole 8′ × 17′ shell is one sauna — the biggest sauna-only room Sweaty Yeti sells, seating 8–12. It's $17,200 more than the base Sauna ($29,700) and $500 more than the Sauna Plus ($46,400), which trades sauna space for a changing room. It also has the highest power demand in the lineup: 75 amps on the order page, 70 in the manual.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to install a Sweaty Yeti Sauna Max?

The Sauna Max unit is $46,900, verified Nov 25, 2026. Delivery within 250 miles is included and jack-system placement adds $250, so the verifiable floor is $47,150 before the 5% coupon ($44,805 after it). The pad and the 240V electrical run are quoted locally and vary by site, so there is no single published installed total.

Is delivery included with the Sauna Max?

Delivery within 250 miles is included on the product page. Beyond 250 miles it is a personalized quote. Placement needs a level gravel or concrete pad and truck + trailer access (+$250); a crane, if needed, is extra and priced TBD.

What electrical service does the Sauna Max need?

A 240V circuit. The order page lists 75 amps; the owner's manual lists 70 amps. Sweaty Yeti's own documents disagree, so have your electrician confirm yours with Sweaty Yeti.

Do I need a concrete pad for a Sweaty Yeti?

Yes — a level gravel or concrete pad, which you arrange before delivery. The pad cost is not published by Sweaty Yeti and varies by site; get a local quote.

What makes a generator RV-ready

Three things, in order. First, a TT-30R 30-amp RV outlet — without it you are adapting a household plug and hoping. Second, clean inverter power, so the RV’s converter, TV, and control boards are safe. Third, noise: campgrounds enforce quiet hours, and anything much over 65 dBA at camp distance draws complaints. The Champion 6500W checks all three at 64 dBA. A conventional open-frame generator can match the watts for less money, but it fails the second and third tests — that is why it is not the RV pick.
